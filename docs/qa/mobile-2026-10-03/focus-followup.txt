CRM keyboard and post-save follow-up, 2026-10-03

Root cause: CrmWorkspace manually selected every button/input/select/textarea/link,
including controls inside closed details, and omitted summary. It also unmounted
the drawer without returning focus and retained the old tab/search after creation.

Changes: CrmDialog uses native showModal for background inertness and initial focus.
A boundary handler wraps Tab/Shift+Tab through visible, enabled controls, including
summary and excluding closed details contents. On dismissal, the triggering button
receives focus (fallback: Add lead). Background scroll is restored on unmount.
Successful manual intake switches to Leads and clears the previous search.

Observed in Codex browser, local synthetic data:
- Closed conversion block: Shift+Tab from Close -> summary; Tab -> Close.
- Open conversion block: Shift+Tab from Close -> Create order; Tab -> Close.
- Escape with unmodified card: closes; focus returns to the original lead button.
- At 390x844, create from Orders with a nonmatching search: successful save opens
  Leads, clears search, shows the new record, focuses Add lead.
- Screenshot: /private/tmp/bleskpro-focus-saved.jpg.

Not claimed: screen-reader certification, all browsers, physical device keyboard,
200% zoom, or complete accessibility compliance. The separate phone-error wording
and inline field feedback issue from the audit remains to be improved.
